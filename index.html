The problem

Payroll assurance is still too fragmented.

Payroll teams already perform extensive checks before approval. The problem is that those checks often live across spreadsheets, reports, source files, inboxes, payroll-provider outputs and individual knowledge.

How it works

One assurance layer around your payroll process.

Preflight does not replace your payroll software. It sits around the calculation process and helps teams understand what should happen, what actually happened and what needs attention before approval.

Who it is for

For payroll teams that want more confidence around sign-off.

Preflight is initially being developed for UK in-house payroll teams where payroll has enough scale and complexity that manual assurance becomes difficult to manage consistently.

It is particularly relevant for teams dealing with regular payroll change, variable inputs, multiple source documents, external payroll providers, system transitions or growing assurance and governance requirements.
