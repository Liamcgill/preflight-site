A different approach to payroll preparation and assurance

Payroll starts before payroll.

Payroll software calculates what it receives. But what if the inputs are missing, conflicting or unapproved? Preflight is exploring a better way to prepare inputs, assure readiness and verify draft results, with traceable evidence and accountable decisions across the workflow.

Prepare is our emerging direction for organising fragmented payroll inputs and mapping them into a controlled dataset, with human review where needed.

Core + Verify are the proposed pre-calculation and post-draft assurance stages.

Evidence would preserve what was received, checked, changed and authorised. Assist would help teams investigate findings without making decisions for them.

The problem

The payroll engine is only part of the story.

Before anyone presses Calculate, payroll teams are chasing instructions, reconciling spreadsheets, resolving conflicts and asking whether anything is missing. After calculation, they are checking whether the result makes sense. Much of that work still lives across inboxes, reports and individual expertise.

The proposed journey

Upload once. Prepare. Assure. Resolve. Export.

Our longer-term vision connects preparation, checks and human decisions around your existing payroll engine. Evidence would link original sources, control assessments, exception resolutions and approved versions throughout. The sequence below is a product direction being tested, not a deployed end-to-end workflow.

Evidence · proposed cross-workflow foundation

Every check has a story. Keep the receipts.

A green tick isn't enough. Evidence is being designed to preserve what was received, which controls were applied, what those controls could and could not assess, who investigated the findings and exactly which data version was authorised.

That means an independent reviewer could follow the reasoning behind a readiness or approval decision, rather than having to reconstruct it from scattered spreadsheets and inboxes.

Proposed design principles, not a claim of an operational audit system, certified controls or external audit reliance.

Illustrative Evidence record · salary change

From conflicting instruction to approved draft

01 · Original sources

HR change file: salary increase effective 1 September. Manager email: effective 15 September. Both originals and versions linked to the employee and period.

02 · Control assessment

Effective-date conflict flagged. Record the control ID, rule version, data version, result, assessment scope and any unassessed areas.

03 · Human resolution

Payroll requests clarification; the authorised decision-maker confirms 15 September. Supporting evidence, reviewer identity and decision time are retained.

04 · Rerun and sign-off

The corrected draft is reassessed. The authorised approver signs off the identified draft version; earlier approval does not silently carry forward.

Assist · proposed support workflow

Keep the payroll expertise in the loop.

Automation can surface a problem. Experienced payroll people still need to understand it, investigate it and make accountable decisions. Assist is our proposed way to explain findings, connect relevant evidence, help request missing information and preserve the reasoning behind resolutions.

Assist would not independently alter payroll data, clear a finding or approve a payroll. Suggestions would require authorised human review.
